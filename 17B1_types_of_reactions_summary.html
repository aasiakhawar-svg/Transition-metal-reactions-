17B.1 Different Types of Reactions

Edexcel IAL Chemistry · An illustrated summary, explained by your teacher

Hello, everyone! Today we solve a colour mystery: why do transition metal solutions change colour when they react? The secret is that only four kinds of change can happen to a complex ion. Any one of them can change the colour, and some reactions do more than one at once. Let’s meet them!
⚡ 1 Redoxoxidation number changes
➖ 2 Deprotonationa ligand loses H⁺
🔄 3 Ligand exchangeone ligand replaced by another
🔢 4 Coordination numbernumber of ligands changes
